Our Story

Forbes Cosmetics began in 2015 with one simple idea: makeup should work for real people and real skin. What started with a single lipstick line has grown into face, eye, and lip products for every part of your routine.

Our Mission

Makeup should feel welcoming, accessible, and fun. We're always working to offer more shades and formulas, so it's easier to find products that feel right for you.
